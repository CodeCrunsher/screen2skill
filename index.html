About the Initiative

What is Screen2Skill?

A student-led exploration into the transformative power of technology in education and personal growth.

🎯

The Mission

To uncover data-backed insights into how digital screens — when used intentionally — become powerful catalysts for developing real-world skills among students aged 14–16.

🚀

The Challenge

Screen2Skill is an AI-powered website and data insights challenge under the TechBuzz competition. Teams of 4 students research, analyze, and present meaningful findings on digital engagement.

Online Mode
4 Students per Team
AI Integration Required
📊

The Approach

We collected survey data, analyzed learning patterns, integrated an AI chatbot, and built interactive visualizations to tell the story of digital empowerment.

Competition Theme

"Online & Optimistic"

Digital tools are not distractions — they are the new classrooms, studios, labs, and libraries for the next generation.

Research Summary Report

Compiled findings from our Screen2Skill data collection and analysis

2026 Report
01

Correlation Between Structured Screen Time and Academic Improvement

Students who use screens with intent (educational apps, structured tutorials) for 2–4 hours daily show a statistically meaningful improvement in academic performance compared to unstructured usage groups.

02

Collaborative Tools Drive Communication Skill Growth

Platforms like Google Workspace, Discord study groups, and GitHub show strong positive associations with student-reported improvements in written and verbal communication abilities.

03

Creative Expression Thrives in Digital Environments

Access to design tools, music production software, and video editing platforms correlates with a 67% increase in students describing themselves as "creatively active."

04

AI Tools Are Becoming Learning Partners

Over 58% of surveyed students report using AI assistants for homework help, research, or skill building — representing a paradigm shift in how the next generation approaches learning.
